Latency and Thermal#

The controller always operates on a stale picture of the road. At \(v = 22\) m/s a delay of \(0.23\) s is already ~5 m of travel. Without lookahead (fp_steer_delay_s) the loop systematically steers for where the car was, not where it is.

Предупреждение

Raw controls/steer contains chassis republishes with stale vision_ts. For e2e use the filter in latency.py (same idea as PlotJuggler latency/ columns).

phone/stats#

At 1 Hz the bag can carry cpu_pct, cpu_app_pct, thermal_status, temperatures, cpu0_freq_khz. Align them with latency/vision/* in PlotJuggler when you claim “thermal”, not “bad MPC”.

Report requirements (course)#

  1. vision_traffic: false when comparing controllers.

  2. phone_stats: true on teaching runs.

  3. Always report Hz and e2e vision alongside CTE.

  4. Do not shrink fp_steer_delay_s to the sum of medians without a closed-loop sweep — dynamics need margin (Vehicle model).
